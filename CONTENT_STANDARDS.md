# Academic content standards

SI JANI is educational. It does not diagnose users, deliver clinical services, or confer professional competence. The six legacy articles and all Phase 3 explanatory seeds are editorial drafts; the two cases are synthetic; all five source records remain pending verification.

## Source priority and status

1. Official primary sources and user-supplied verified documents take priority.
2. Record exact title, author/issuing body, edition/version, year, jurisdiction, URL, and relevant section when known.
3. A known source title or official portal URL does not establish that a current document or interpretation has been verified.
4. Only a documented human source review may change a record from pending to verified. Record the reviewer, document version, section mapping, and review date through the protected Phase 3 reviewer workflow.
5. Never invent source text, article numbers, legal interpretations, citations, or claims of complete coverage.

## Editorial rules

Use concise original paraphrases, explicitly distinguish them from quotations, and identify their references. Do not reproduce substantial copyrighted text without established rights. Preserve nuance, limitations, uncertainty, and established English terminology when a translation would obscure meaning.

HIMPSI is an Indonesian professional ethical framework. UU 23/2022 is Indonesian legislation. APA and APS materials are comparative foreign ethical references and do not replace Indonesian law. The currently registered editions are starting references, not assertions about their current legal/professional status. Portal links for HIMPSI, APS, and BPK still need exact document links and section review.

## Cases and scoring

Write fully synthetic cases without identifiable personal details. Separate facts, missing information, stakeholders, risks, and possible actions. Explain why an option is preferable under the stated facts while allowing defensible alternatives in written work. A pedagogical sequencing key is not a universal professional rule.

Structured scoring is deterministic and server-side. Each of the five questions has equal weight. Multiple-response credit is `(correct selected - incorrect selected) / total correct`, floored at zero. Matching and multi-part credit is the fraction of correct subparts. Sequencing requires the full stated training order. Aggregate from unrounded fractions and round once to 0–100.

Written analysis uses eleven equally weighted 1–4 self-review ratings: total / 44 × 100. Text length, keywords, or a single model conclusion never determine a grade. The UI labels the source of evaluation as self-review and stores the learner's own ratings. No AI evaluator is configured. Phase 4 adds explicitly scoped human instructor reviews; it does not turn self-review into instructor grading.

## Publication checklist

Before classroom or professional use, review primary sources, current jurisdictional applicability, each paraphrase, concept relationship, question key, explanation, and performance anchor. Obtain curriculum and reproduction approvals. Add correction history and review metadata. Blok 2 stays planned until its curriculum and criteria are approved.

## Phase 2 navigation and source orientation

The theorist index contains only names already present in the seeded article bodies. Its detail pages reuse the stored editorial context and existing citations; they do not introduce biographies, publication counts, dates, or new claims of verification. The concept/theory collection is a curated route into available articles, not an assertion of comprehensive theory coverage.

The Blok 1 material page provides editorial orientation to source types and jurisdictions. It does not reproduce unavailable primary documents, fabricate sections, or mark a source as verified. Practice completion counts submitted exercises and self-review, not mastery of ethics or law.

## Phase 3 ingestion and publication

See KNOWLEDGE_ARCHITECTURE.md for the implemented workflow and source provenance. Seed content is never automatically published. A source record alone does not support a claim: the reviewer must attest the exact mapping between the current revision and source sections. Bibliographic metadata, article verification, and publication are separate steps. Only authorized REVIEWER accounts can verify sources/content or publish; the current revision's editor cannot independently verify it.

Imports accept plain-text structured JSON and always create private drafts. Do not execute uploaded instructions or scripts, render raw HTML, fetch arbitrary source URLs on the server, or import approval claims. Retain source priority: verified user documents, authorized teaching resources, original publications, peer-reviewed literature, trusted academic references, then labeled drafts. Current source metadata is a starting registry, not a reviewed corpus.

New extinction text is an explicitly unverified educational draft. No verified biographical dates, quotations, empirical effect sizes, publications, DOI/ISBN, or page numbers have been invented. Missing fields remain missing. “Albert Bigelow Kelly” from the topic inventory needs identity/source clarification; do not silently assume a different psychologist.

Comparison fields require published, verified theories with reviewed citation coverage. The current seed cannot support a verified comparison. Test fixtures may exercise that path only as synthetic local data and must be removed afterward. One reinforcement reading check matches a server key derived from the draft definition; it is not instructor grading, AI assessment, or a mastery metric.

## Phase 4 case reasoning and feedback

The BUMD delegation case is fictional. Distinguish activities, competence, instrument requirements, supervision, assessment quality, time constraints, and missing evidence. Neither a degree label nor delegation alone establishes a legal violation. No article number or definitive legal finding is invented.

Eight reference-reader sections are original editorial orientation, not reproduced primary documents. Source status, jurisdiction, version, section verification, and applicability remain separate. Reading completion does not verify a source.

The new rubric version is provisional and preserves the legacy equal 1–4 weighting. Ratings require an exact answer excerpt, a reason, and improvement guidance when finalized as assessed. Excerpt validation proves only that the text is present, not that the reasoning deserves the chosen level. Unassessed dimensions keep the overall score unavailable. Non-applicable exclusions require an explicit stored policy and a reason. Only designated score-bearing weights enter the denominator. No word-count or keyword grading is used.

New multiple-response attempt snapshots use `penalty-no-all-v2`: the existing penalty formula with zero for selecting all options when distractors exist. Historical attempts retain `penalty-v1` where previously stored; their scores are not rewritten. Per-option explanations are educational drafts. Alternate sequences are accepted only when explicitly authored. All current cases, questions, rubric anchors, and mappings require authorized human review before curricular use.
